# High-Conversion Cold Outreach Playbook for Indian High Finance
### Tailored for CSE Undergrads targeting Summer 2027 Internships

---

## 1. Golden Rules of Indian High Finance Outreach
1. **Never reach out to Managing Directors (MDs) or Partners first**: They get 200+ emails a week and will archive your mail. Reach out to **Analysts (1-2 yrs experience)** and **Associates (3-4 yrs experience)**. They are in the trenches, review intern tests, and can refer you directly to the deal team.
2. **Never send a generic "Looking for an internship" message**: Always lead with a specific insight or an attached artifact (e.g. 1-page financial model or memo).
3. **Keep the ask low-friction**: Don't ask for a job or an interview. Ask for a **10-minute informational chat** or **feedback on your valuation model**.
